One important lesson I learned throughout my coursework is that developing software involves more than writing code. It also requires communication, teamwork, and understanding what users and stakeholders need. During my coursework involving Agile development and Scrum, I learned about the importance of working together, sharing ideas, completing tasks, and communicating progress with a team. These experiences helped me understand how different roles contribute to the success of a project. I also learned that technical information needs to be explained clearly so that people with different levels of technical knowledge can understand the purpose of a solution. As I continue developing my career, I want to use these skills to work effectively with others, listen to feedback, and contribute to projects that meet organizational goals.

My ePortfolio demonstrates my growth in software design and engineering, algorithms and data structures, and databases. Enhancing this project helped me focus on organizing code, improving functionality, and making the application easier to maintain. I also developed a better understanding of how algorithms and data structures help applications process information and solve problems efficiently. My database enhancement allowed me to focus on how applications organize, retrieve, and manage information. These experiences showed me that a successful application must do more than work correctly. ## Security and Professional Growth

Security has also become an important part of how I think about software development. Applications can contain information that needs to be protected, so developers must consider authentication, access controls, input validation, and the possibility of unauthorized access. My experience working with application functionality and security concepts helped me understand why security should be considered throughout the development process instead of added only at the end. I also learned the importance of testing changes to make sure they work as expected and do not create new problems. These skills have helped me become more aware of the responsibility developers have when building software that people depend on.
